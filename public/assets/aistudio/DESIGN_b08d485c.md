## Brand & Style

This design system channels the uncompromising engineering, aerodynamic drama, and visceral prestige of hypercar design, drawing direct lineage from Maranello track culture and modern aerodynamic telemetry. It balances pitch-black minimalism with razor-sharp computational accents to evoke raw velocity, high-calibre luxury, and technical mastery.

### Aesthetics & Tone
- **Atmosphere:** Dark luxury, clinical precision, trackside command center.
- **Visual Style:** Technical Automotive Luxury combined with Dark Bento modularity. Surfaces feel like forged carbon, ceramic brake heat, and antireflective cockpit instrumentation.
- **Emotion:** Exclusivity, controlled aggression, speed, and absolute mechanical precision.
- **Contrast Philosophy:** Extreme black voids punctuated by razor-thin translucent boundaries and searing bursts of high-velocity scarlet.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around pure visual depth and telemetry-grade hierarchy. Backgrounds progress outward from absolute pitch black into subtly stepped metallic-tinted carbon plates, illuminated by genuine Rosso Corsa accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas Base (`#050505`):** The primary canvas absorption field. No ambient grey light; pure, deep negative space.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#0E0E10`):** Secondary structural planes, side rails, sticky instrumentation docks, and persistent headers.
- **Bento Card Surface (`#131316`):** The standard container plane for interactive components and telemetry tiles.
- **Rosso Corsa Accent (`#FF2800`):** Reserved strictly for high-priority telemetry triggers, active states, aerodynamic markers, and track-line indicators.
- **Accent Glow (`rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.22)`):** Diffuse radiant aura applied selectively behind active selectors and tachymeter bars.
- **Outlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`):** Hairline precision borders defining modules without heavy visual mass.
- **Hover Structural Outlines (`rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.45)`):** Dynamic reactive boundary transitions indicating immediate mechanical readiness.

### Text Contrast Tiers
- **Ink Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** Unadulterated stark white for primary headline typography and vital machine stats.
- **Ink Muted (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.58)`):** Readouts, specifications, metadata, and architectural secondary descriptions.
- **Ink Faint (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.30)`):** Inactive track positions, grid indices, monospaced tick marks, and structural timestamps.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between muscular geometric modernism (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) and raw digital cockpit instrumentation (`JetBrains Mono`).

### Font Pairing Strategy
- **Headlines & Editorial Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides high geometric density with ultra-tight tracking for bold headlines, mimicking the streamlined aerodynamics and exterior panels of performance supercars.
- **Telemetry, Specs & Meta:** `JetBrains Mono` handles high-precision data readouts, lap splits, chassis numbers, RPM calculations, and technical nomenclature. All badges, tags, and data units must be rendered in this monospaced family with uppercase transformation.
- **Micro-Hierarchy:** Always pair large display typography directly adjacent to micro-scale telemetry tokens (e.g., a massive `display-xl` horsepower statistic backed by a subtle `label-caption` unit designation).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetrical, dense Bento architecture within a rigid 12-column responsive fluid grid.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Modular Bento Grid:** Panels are arranged in high-density interlocking grids. Components favor widescreen horizontal aspect ratios (16:9, 21:9) and structured 2-column or 3-column asymmetric configurations (e.g., an 8-column vehicle schematic paired with a 4-column live telemetry feed).
- **Outer Canvas Margins:** On desktop environments, `margin` enforces 48px (`3rem`) of breathing room from the viewport edges. On mobile devices, this drops to 20px (`1.25rem`).
- **Inner Module Padding:** Standard cards employ `space-lg` (24px) internally, scaling down to `space-md` (16px) on compact handheld displays.
- **Data Gap Rhythm:** For adjacent metrics and telemetry readouts, maintain compact horizontal distribution using `space-xs` and `space-sm` to maintain digital cockpit cohesion.

## Elevation & Depth

This system discards conventional fuzzy drop shadows in favor of layered material opacity, hairline luminescence, and technical optical glows.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Floor (L0):** Pitch black canvas (`#050505`) with zero elevation.
- **Deck Level (L1):** Sub-surfaces (`#0E0E10`) with sharp 1px borders colored `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active Card Tier (L2):** Modular bento plates (`#131316`) overlaid with a 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- **Focused / Hover State (L3):** Border shifts to `rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.45)` accompanied by an ambient under-panel glow: `0 0 32px -4px rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.22)`.

### Depth Techniques
- **Linear Light Bleeds:** Gradients used across borders to suggest directional light bouncing off carbon edges (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.18) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.02) 100%)`).
- **Telemetry Underglow:** Active elements, selected chassis profiles, and accelerator gauges cast a sharp, short-throw backlight utilizing `Accent Glow`.

## Shapes

Shapes emulate laser-cut composite components and precision vehicle chassis work.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Corner Architecture:** Default radius is set to Level 1 (Soft / 4px base). Bento cards and elevated dashboards strictly utilize `rounded-lg` (8px / `0.5rem`). Avoid round, pill-shaped, or bubbly silhouettes.
- **Micro Radii:** Badges, small tags, tooltips, and status indicators strictly employ 2px to 4px boundaries to maintain an industrial, machined presence.
- **Telemetry Dividers:** Hairline vertical or horizontal dividers are exactly 1px thick with alpha drop-off at their extremities (`linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255,255,255,0.1), transparent)`).

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Controls
- **Primary Action (Rosso Trigger):** Background `#FF2800`, text `#FFFFFF` (`Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold), border none. Hover triggers a concentrated halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Background transparent, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, text `#FFFFFF`. On hover, the border shifts to `rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.45)` and surface tints to `rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Telemetry Action Button:** Monospace, all-caps, with an inline directional indicator (e.g., `SYS.ENGAGE ->`).

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- **Chassis & Status Badges:** Rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-badge`), uppercase, tracked wide (`0.08em`). Background `#0E0E10`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`.
- **Live Indicator Badges:** Incorporate a glowing 6px circular LED indicator in `#FF2800` pulsing at 2-second intervals.

### Bento Cards
- **Construction:** Background `#131316`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, border radius 8px (`rounded-lg`). Padding `space-lg`.
- **Hover Micro-Interactions:** 1px border transition to `rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.45)` with ambient inner luminescence. 

### Telemetry Readouts & Data Displays
- **Key-Value Pairs:** Labels rendered in `label-caption` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.58)`), values set in `telemetry-lg` or `telemetry-md` (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Accent Striping:** Critical specs (e.g., Top Speed, 0-100 km/h, Torque) integrate an absolute 2px vertical crimson indicator anchor along the card’s left flank.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- **Cockpit Input Fields:** Dark flat base `#0E0E10`, hairline outline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.30)`. Focus transition triggers an exact 1px border in `#FF2800` with an accompanying subtle glow (`rgba(255, 40, 0, 0.22)`). Monospaced metadata prefixes (e.g., `SEARCH //`).

### Checkboxes, Radios & Switches
- **Toggle Components:** Squared off, 2px border radius. Inactive tracks: `#0E0E10` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)` outline. Active state switches immediately to solid `#FF2800` fill with white mechanical interior ticks.
